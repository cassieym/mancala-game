# Mancala
A 2-player Java version of the mancala game with capture feature.

## Game Rules
GOAL: Collect more stones in your store than your opponent.

INFO:
- The vertical rectangles on the left and right outer sides of the board are called 'stores'
- Each player has 6 pits and 1 store
- Player 1's store is on the left
- Player 1's pits are on the top row
- Player 2's store is on the right
- Each pit starts with 4 stones

BASIC RULES:
- Click on one of your pits (must contain stones)
- Stones are distributed counter-clockwise, one per pit
- You place stones in your own store and skip opponent's store
- The game ends when one player's pits are all empty. 
- All of the remaining stones go the other player's store
- 5. The player with the most stones in their store wins

SPECIAL RULES:
- EXTRA TURN: If the last stone lands in your store, you get another turn\n" +
- CAPTURE: If the last stone lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture that stone AND all opponent's stones in the opposite pit. All captured stones go to your store.

CONTROLS:
- Reset: Start a new game
- Undo: Go back to your last move
- Save: Save current game state
- Load: Load previously saved game
